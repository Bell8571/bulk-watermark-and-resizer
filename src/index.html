How to use this tool

Watermark and resize a whole batch of photos in your browser — nothing is uploaded to a server. Follow the steps below.

  1. Upload your images Drag & drop files onto the upload box, or click Select Images to browse. Up to 50 images per batch, max 50 MB each.
  2. Configure resize & watermark Choose resize mode (dimensions or percentage), toggle between text/image watermark, pick position from the 3x3 grid, and adjust opacity/color/scale.
  3. Live Preview See real-time preview of the first image with current settings applied on the live canvas.
  4. Process & Export Click Process Images. Progress bar shows status. Download as individual files or a single ZIP archive.

Tips

  • Client-side only: all processing happens in your browser using Canvas API.
  • Supports JPEG, PNG, WebP, GIF, BMP. Max 50 files, 50MB each.
  • Use the 3x3 position grid for precise watermark placement.
  • Image watermarks (logos) maintain aspect ratio automatically.
